Code Form remain governed by MPL 2.0. Separate files in a Larger Work may be
licensed under other terms, subject to the MPL and any applicable third-party
licences.

rights.

The current third-party tool matrix is documented in
[`docs/LEGAL_THIRD_PARTY.md`](docs/LEGAL_THIRD_PARTY.md).

Licensing grants already made for historical QEC releases are not revoked.
Copies obtained under an earlier licence may continue to be used under the terms
that accompanied those copies. The MPL 2.0 repository default applies to the
repository state from this change forward.

## Contributions

By submitting a contribution to this repository, a contributor agrees that the
contribution is made available under MPL 2.0 and represents that they have
sufficient rights to grant that licence. Contributors must preserve applicable
copyright, patent, attribution and third-party licence notices.

New QEC-authored source files should use:

```text
SPDX-License-Identifier: MPL-2.0
```

The comment syntax should match the file format. Files that cannot reasonably
carry a header are covered by the repository-level `LICENSE` and this policy.
